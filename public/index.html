Our Mission: Your Safety

Every woman deserves to feel safe on her journey. PathSafe was built to change the way we think about navigation – it's not just about the fastest route, it's about the safest one.

Our AI analyzes millions of data points every second: crime patterns, street lighting quality, pedestrian traffic density, proximity to emergency services, and even time-of-day risk factors. We don't just show you where danger is – we guide you around it before you ever get close.

How PathSafe Keeps You Safe

🗺️

Real-Time Safety Analysis

As you plan your route, PathSafe instantly analyzes crime statistics, recent incidents, street lighting conditions, and pedestrian traffic. Our AI assigns a safety score to every possible path and recommends the safest option.

⚠️

Danger Zone Alerts

If you're approaching an area with elevated risk, PathSafe sends you an immediate alert and suggests alternative routes. You'll never accidentally walk into an unsafe area again.

🚨

Emergency Access Optimization

Every route is evaluated for proximity to police stations, hospitals, and emergency services. PathSafe ensures help is always nearby if you need it.

💡

Smart Lighting Detection

Walking at night? PathSafe prioritizes well-lit streets and avoids dark alleys or poorly illuminated areas. Our system knows which streets are bright and which ones to avoid after dark.

Powerful Safety Features

🎯

Intelligent Route Planning

Our AI doesn't just find the shortest path – it finds the safest. Every route considers crime data, lighting, traffic density, and emergency access to keep you protected.

📊

Live Risk Scoring

See real-time safety scores for every route. Our dynamic scoring system updates continuously based on time of day, weather, and recent incidents in the area.

👥

Community-Powered Alerts

Women helping women. Report incidents, share safety tips, and receive alerts from other PathSafe users in your area. Together, we're safer.

🌙

Night Mode Safety

Extra protection when you need it most. Night mode prioritizes well-lit streets, busy areas, and routes with higher police presence.

⚡

Instant Notifications

🔒

Privacy First
